# Storybook 场景素材

本目录包含三张原创预置封面插图，供在 Storybook 中创作与编辑短篇故事时选用。图片由 Codex 内置 ImageGen 生成原始 PNG，在不改动构图的前提下转换为 JPEG（质量 87%）随附打包。这些插图属于静态内置素材，并非应用在运行时动态生成；应用运行时的图像生成仍遵循 Nimi App Access 的能力边界。

## harbor.jpg

Use case: illustration-story. Create an original cinematic landscape illustration for a literary interactive mystery titled 雾港来信. Asset is a wide hero background in a desktop story library (display 1000 by 420) and an atmospheric scene header. Old fogbound harbor at blue hour: a solitary lighthouse on the RIGHT THIRD emits a small warm amber light through voluminous pale teal fog, aged fishing village roof silhouettes beneath, quiet wet stone jetty receding diagonally from bottom center, one distant solitary investigator silhouette. LEFT HALF is mostly atmospheric dark petrol blue water and fog, low detail for cream text overlay. Restrained painterly oil and subtle grain, beautifully composed premium illustrated book cover, tactile editorial art, deep teal, desaturated slate, tiny warm honey light. Full bleed, no text, no typography, no border, no watermark. Landscape 1536x1024.
